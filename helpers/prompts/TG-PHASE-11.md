# Phase 11 — Usefulness pass, step 2: real starts and evenings, researched place facts, season and local favourites, the brochure, then outlines and day versions

> Written 2026-10-03 by the coordinator of the owner's usefulness review. This phase is the review's **step 2**: suggestions 3, 4 and 7, then 13 (a brochure design pass for the new facts), then 8 in the form the owner chose — "Outlines + days": two or three whole-journey outlines to pick or mix, then two or three versions of each day compared in the Mini App, and one brochure for the chosen mix. Step 3 follows as Phase 12. Model rule (owner, 2026-10-03): coordinator Opus 5.5, builders `hb-builder-opus` (Opus 5.5 · high), **no Fable anywhere**. Progress in `helpers/BUILD-STATE.md`.

Phase 10 made the day card trustworthy. Step 2 makes every new day worth having. Today a day that begins with a journey still starts at the lodging at the trip's day start; every dinner is "near <lodging>" (`planner/planner-day.mjs:247-248`) while saved restaurants wait in the Later list; an early finish is only "back early; the rest of the day is free" (`:246`); visit lengths are category guesses and the schedule trusts Google's closing time over a place's own last entry; nothing knows the season, so out-of-season gardens pass and evening light-ups are missed; two local mentions only lower the star floor to the default (`gems/gems-screen.mjs:91`), so quiet local favourites are thrown away; and crowd magnets get whatever slot the solver finds. Then the owner wants options to compare rather than one plan to approve.

The phase runs in **two waves**. Wave 1 (suggestions 3, 4, 7 and 13): four work packages in parallel, each in its own worktree; the coordinator merges them, wires the cross-WP calls, runs every check and pushes once, then opens the private repo's PR (WP-11g). Wave 2 (suggestion 8 and the day versions) starts after the wave-1 push: two work packages on top of Contract C11, then the same merge, push and private PR (WP-11h).

## Contract C11 — new optional fields

Every field is optional, so old trips, places, digests, the private repo's old pin and the live core keep working. Bounds are exact: a validator accepts a field only with the type and limits below, and still rejects unknown keys. Times are `HH:MM`, dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, URLs https only.

**Inputs** (trip and place memory, and the planner's input):

| Where | Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| trip | `day_overrides` | array ≤ 31 of `{ date, start?, end?, day_start?, day_end?, bags?, bags_note? ≤ 160, note? ≤ 200 }`; dates unique and inside the trip; `day_end` at least 2 hours after the day's start | Days with their own hours, or that do not start or end at the lodging |
| day override | `day_start`, `day_end` | time | That day's hours, in place of the trip's (the day still starts and ends at the lodging unless `start`/`end` say otherwise; with `start`, its `time` wins) |

# Wave 1 — suggestions 3, 4, 7 and 13

## WP-11a — Planner: real starts, dinners, evenings, facts in the schedule, crowd timing · `hb-builder-opus`

**Paths you own.** `helpers/packs/tour-guide/planner/*`, `estimator/*`, `fixtures/*` (add invented fixtures: a moving day, a dinner pool, a season sheet, places with facts); the schemas `tour-guide-day-plan.schema.json` and `tour-guide-plan.schema.json` (JSON only — a change needed in `schemas/index.mjs` or `tour-guide-checks.mjs` is a REQUEST); the planner and estimator tests; new test files.

**Evidence.** `buildDays` (`planner/planner-input.mjs:35-44`) starts and ends every day at a lodging, at the trip's `day_start` and `day_end`. Dinner is always `at: 'lodging'`, "near <lodging>", at the later of 30 minutes after the finish and 18:30 (`planner-day.mjs:247-248`); lunch already picks a place near a stop. An early finish becomes one free block (`:246`). A visit's length is the estimator's (`estimator/estimator-defaults.mjs:5-10`: one default per category, no country), and a stop is fitted inside Google's opening window (`planner-day.mjs:188-192`), so a place whose last entry is 30 minutes before closing can be scheduled too late.

**Build.**
- Day anchors: for a date in `trip.day_overrides`, `day_start`/`day_end` replace the trip's hours for that day; `start` replaces the morning lodging as the day's first point and its `time` replaces the day's start (no breakfast at the lodging); `end` does the same for the evening. The reserved slugs `day-start` and `day-end` name those points in legs. Bags: `hotel` sends the day to the night's lodging first (a 15-minute stop, "leave your bags") before the first sight; `locker` adds a 10-minute stop at the start point and ends the day through the start point to collect them; `forward` and `carry` add only the day's `bags.text` ("bags sent ahead to <lodging>"). Put `start`, `end` and `bags` on the DayPlan.
- Dinner: from `input.dinners` choose a place open for the dinner window that day, within about 1.5 km of the last stop or the night's lodging (pick the radius and record it), not used for dinner on another day, and never one whose `facts.menu.fits` is `no`; prefer the owner's ✅ picks, then the Later list. The legs to dinner and back are honest legs under the Phase 10 rules (count the extra route requests against the day's budget and record it). Dinner's `at` is the restaurant's slug; its `booking` comes from `trip.bookings` (matching `for_date` and `place`) or `facts.booking`. If nothing fits, keep today's "near <lodging>" dinner. Dinner places go into `plan.places`. The planner never adds a booking.
- Evening extras: on every day, season events (`trip.season.events`) running that evening within about 2 km in a straight line of the last stop or the lodging; when the day ends at least 60 minutes before `day_end`, also saved places open then within the same radius. At most 3, events first; when extras exist they replace the "back early" line.
- Sunset: a pure function (the NOAA solar algorithm, no network) gives each day's `sunset` at the night's lodging in the trip's zone. Test it on both hemispheres, within 2 minutes of reference values whose source you record.
- Facts in the schedule: a stop must start by `facts.last_entry` and end by `facts.close` when given (else Google's close); `closed_weekdays` closes the place on those days; `facts.visit_minutes` sets the length (`minutes_source: 'official'`; a researched estimate is `research`; a category default is `estimate`); the stop carries `last_entry`. When the place's own hours disagree with Google's, use its own and add an info warning.
- Country-aware defaults: `estimator-defaults.mjs` gains per-country overrides keyed by the trip's `country`, Japan first (temples, shrines, gardens, tea ceremonies, course meals). Record each number with its source.
- Crowd timing: when the profile excerpt's `avoid` asks to avoid peak hours or crowds (pick the matching rule and record it), a stop flagged `crowd_magnet` is placed within 60 minutes of its opening or in the last 90 minutes before its effective close, and carries `crowd_slot`. When no such slot fits, keep the stop where it fits and add a warning; never drop a place because of this rule.

**Tests.** A moving day starts at the override's point and time, with each `bags` kind; a per-date `day_start`/`day_end` changes only that day; a day without an override is unchanged. Dinner picks the near, open, diet-compatible place, never `fits: no`, never the same place on two days, and falls back to the old line. Extras: an evening event near the route, a saved place on an early finish, never more than 3. Sunset within 2 minutes of the references. A stop with a last entry before Google's close starts by the last entry; `closed_weekdays` moves or drops it; `visit_minutes` sets its length and `minutes_source`. Country defaults apply only in that country. A crowd slot with the profile rule and none without it. An old fixture plans exactly as before.

## WP-11b — Research inputs: place facts, the season sheet, local favourites, crowd magnets · `hb-builder-opus`

**Paths you own.** `helpers/packs/tour-guide/gems/*`; new modules `helpers/packs/tour-guide/facts/*` and `season/*`; the schemas `tour-guide-place.schema.json` and `tour-guide-trip.schema.json`; in `schemas/tour-guide-checks.mjs` only `checkTrip` and `checkPlace` (lines 13–59; add any helper next to them); the gems tests; new test files.

**Evidence.** Two mentions from distinct publishers (`mentionCount`, `gems/gems-record.mjs:175-177`) only lower the floor to the default 4.3 (`gems/gems-screen.mjs:91`), and the off-track limit is the same for every place (`:95`), so a quiet 4.1-star place the local press loves is dropped as `low_rating`. Nothing knows bloom seasons, so an out-of-season rose or hydrangea garden still passes. Places carry no researched facts: no last entry, booking rule, price or menu check.

**Build.**
- `facts/`: `normalizeFacts(raw)` → a valid `place.facts` or errors; `factsLines(facts, { now, diet })` → `{ facts_line, booking_line, price_line, menu_checked }`, each ≤ 160 (for example "Last entry 16:00 · about 60–90 min · official site", "Book 2 days ahead by phone · 2 people or more", "€25 set lunch, garden entry included"); `factsStale(facts, now)` (pick the ages, for example 90 days for facts and 30 for a menu check, and record them); `factsConflict(facts, googleHours, date)`.
- `season/`: `normalizeSeason(raw)` → a valid `trip.season` or errors; `eventsOn(season, date)`; `bloomOn(season, date)` → a short line; `outOfSeason(place, { season, date, lat })`: a garden or park whose name or types say it is mainly one bloom (roses, cherry, plum, hydrangea, wisteria, iris, lotus, lavender) is out of season when that bloom's usual months (by hemisphere; pick and record them) miss every trip date, unless `season.bloom` says otherwise.
- Gem screen: a new drop reason `out_of_season` (owner seeds are never dropped). A place with mentions from at least two distinct publishers (a mention without a publisher counts by its ref, as `mentionCount` does) is a `local_favourite`: its floor drops to about 3.8 plus the country's offset, its off-track limit grows by about half (record both numbers), and it carries the flag. `crowd_magnet` marks a place on a mass-tourism top-ten list or in the top decile of the pool's rating counts above a minimum count (pick and record it). Labels and line words for both flags go next to the existing ones (`gems-flags.mjs`, `gems-line.mjs`). The shortlist item carries `local_favourite: true` (the shortlist schema is WP-11c's).
- Schemas and checks: the trip schema gains `day_overrides` and `season`, the place schema `facts` and the two flags, with C11's bounds. `checkTrip` enforces override dates unique and inside the trip, an overridden day at least 2 hours long, event ids unique and `from ≤ to`; `checkPlace` enforces `visit_minutes.min ≤ max`.

**Tests.** Each normaliser accepts a full invented record and rejects each out-of-bounds value and an unknown key; `factsLines`, staleness and conflict outputs. Out-of-season gardens drop on both hemispheres and stay when `season.bloom` says peak; owner seeds stay. A 4.0-star place with two distinct-publisher mentions passes as a local favourite, and one with two mentions from one publisher does not; crowd magnets on an invented pool. Old trip and place records still validate.
