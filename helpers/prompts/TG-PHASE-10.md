# Phase 10 — Usefulness pass, step 1: the right day abroad, honest travel legs, a planner tidy-up, booking deadlines

> Written 2026-10-03 by the coordinator of the owner's usefulness review (a private report: the owner approved 11 of its 16 suggestions and set the build order). This phase is the review's **step 1** — suggestions 1, 2, 5 and 6. Steps 2 and 3 follow as Phases 11 and 12. Model rule (owner, 2026-10-03): coordinator Opus 5.5, builders `hb-builder-opus` (Opus 5.5 · high), **no Fable anywhere**. Progress in `helpers/BUILD-STATE.md`.

The Tour Guide works end to end (Phases 0–9). A review of what makes a travel agenda useful found four faults that stop the owner trusting the day card on the ground: "today" is the owner's home date, so abroad `/today` and `/replan today` pick the wrong day; legs the planner decides to walk are labelled "transit", lose their estimate mark before the brochure sees them, carry no buffer and never flag a hill; six small planner faults; and nothing tracks booking windows or deadlines. Two work packages run in parallel, each in its own worktree. The coordinator merges them, wires the one cross-WP call that needs both, runs every check and pushes once. The private repo follows after the push and the re-pin (WP-10c, coordinator).

## Contract C10 — new optional fields

Every field is optional, so old digests, the private repo's old pin and the live core keep working. The `plan_digest` payload and the DayPlan use the same names:

| Where | Field | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| plan_digest, top level | `tz` | IANA zone, the pattern of `trip.timezone` | The trip's zone (the DayPlan has none; the trip record does) |
| day | `spare_minutes` | integer 0–1440 | Unscheduled minutes in the day's window after buffers; 0 when the day overflows |
| stop | `time_style` | `"about"` or `"exact"` | Absent means exact. The data keeps exact `arrive`/`depart`; only the display rounds |
| stop | `check_on_day` | string 1–160 | Why to check this stop on the day (irregular or unknown opening days) |
| leg | `estimated` | `true` | Absent means Google's own route. Already in the DayPlan; now kept through `fetchLeg` and carried into the digest |
| leg | `distance_m` | integer 0–500 000 | Already in the DayPlan; now carried into the digest |
| leg | `flags` | array of at most 4 unique values from `"footpath"`, `"trail"`, `"uphill"`, `"downhill"` | The route's character |
| leg | `taxi_minutes` | integer 0–1440 | Driving time for the same leg, given when the walk is up a real hill or on a trail |
| leg | `buffer_minutes` | integer 0–120 | Slack the schedule adds after this leg before the next item starts |

A dropped place's minutes go in the warning or Later text (≤ 200 / ≤ 300 characters); no new field. **WP-10a accepts and stores** the fields (schema JSON, the GAS validator, `Trips`/`DayPlans` storage, `tgDigestDays` output, the snapshot). **WP-10b produces them** (planner, DayPlan schema) **and displays them** (day card, brochure), testing the day card by calling `tgCmdDayMessages` with day objects directly, so neither WP waits for the other. The private repo's digest builder maps them after the push (WP-10c).

**Build (6) — booking deadlines and reminders.**
- **Record** (`tour-guide-booking.schema.json`; the trip schema also allows `bookings[]`, at most 40): `id` slug, unique per trip · `title` 1–120 · `kind` `train|lodging|meal|sight|experience|other` · `rule` 1–200 (party size, how to book, how far ahead, in the owner's words) · `status` `todo|booked|not_needed` · optional: `place` slug, `for_date` date (trip-local), `opens_at` and `book_by` (ISO 8601 with an explicit offset, for example `2027-03-01T10:00:00+13:00`; `opens_at ≤ book_by` when both are given), `how` ≤ 120, `party_min` integer 1–20, `url` https ≤ 2000, `note` ≤ 200, `source` ≤ 120, `updated` date.
- **Envelope** `bookings` (a new type in `helper.json`, with a payload schema and a strict GAS validator; at most 40 records, unique ids): `{ v: 1, kind: 'bookings', trip, tz, bookings: […] }`. The brain's full list for that trip **replaces** the core's, except that **the owner's taps win**: a record the owner marked `booked` or `not_needed` stays so when the brain sends `todo` (the brain may still move a record to `booked` itself). Records missing from the new list are removed. `tz` sets the trip's zone.
- **Store**: a new `Bookings` tab (`trip, id, record_json, status, status_by, snooze_until, last_reminded, alerted_at, updated_at`), created on first use.
- **Show**: `/trip` gains the trip's bookings, each with its rule and **both** times ("opens Mon 1 Mar 10:00 in <destination> · Sun 28 Feb 1:00 pm your time"), done ones folded into a count — put the lines in a function in `14_bookings.js` and call it from `tgCmdTripMessages` with one line. `/bookings` lists every open booking across trips, nearest deadline first. `tgBkDayLines(trip, day) → string[]` gives the day card its lines for bookings whose `for_date` is that day (WP-10b calls it behind a `typeof` guard). All text through `tgEscape`; https URLs only.
- **Remind**: (i) an opening alert about 30 minutes before `opens_at` — Apps Script runs a timed trigger at or after its time, sometimes minutes late; check Google's documentation and never rely on exactness; (ii) one daily reminder at 09:00 in the owner's *current* zone (the trip's while a trip is current, else home), listing the `todo` records whose window is open, nearest deadline first, overdue ones marked; (iii) nothing for a record once it is `booked`, `not_needed` or snoozed. Each reminder line has buttons ✅ Booked · Not needed · Tomorrow (snooze to the next local day): callback prefix `bk` with a short hash key (callback data ≤ 64 bytes); a tap edits the message in place. `/bookings now` sends today's reminder at once (the owner's test).
- **Core**: one generic way for pack code to run at a time — a new registry, for example `registerAlarm(name, { next(nowMs) → ms | null, run(nowMs) })`, and a core handler `alarmTrigger` added to `ONE_OFF_HANDLERS` (`core/12_wake.js`). At most **one** pending alarm trigger: re-armed (delete, then one `timeBased().at()`) after each run, after a `bookings` envelope, after a tap, and by a daily job (the backstop if the trigger was cleared); `clearOneOffTriggers` clears it too. Stay within Apps Script's trigger quota (check the number in Google's documentation). SPEC §5, §16 and §18 updated.
- **Snapshot**: `tour_guide.bookings: { <trip>: [{ id, status, status_by, updated_at }] }`, so routines can sync the owner's taps into memory.
- **Brochure**: `brochure-map/brochure-map-bookings.mjs` exports `bookingsSection(bookings, { tripTz, ownerTz, now })` → a practical section `{ title: 'Bookings', items: [{ label, text, url? }] }` (both times, open ones first) or `null`. The coordinator wires it into the practical page.
- The pack's action allowlist stays empty: the Tour Guide never books anything.

## WP-10b — Planner and display: honest travel legs (2) and the tidy-up (5) · `hb-builder-opus`

**Paths you own.** `helpers/packs/tour-guide/planner/*`, `estimator/*`, `gems/*`, `fixtures/*` (add an invented hill fixture), `brochure-map/*` **except** `brochure-map-bookings.mjs`; `helpers/kits/maps/*` and `helpers/kits/brochure/*`; the schemas `tour-guide-day-plan.schema.json`, `tour-guide-plan.schema.json` and `tour-guide-place.schema.json` (JSON only — a change needed in `schemas/index.mjs` or `tour-guide-checks.mjs` is a REQUEST); in `gas/10_commands.js` only the functions `tgCmdDayMessages` and `tgCmdRainStop` (any helper you add goes next to them, named `tgCmdDay…`); the planner, estimator, gems, maps, brochure and fixture tests; the day-card assertions in `pack_tour-guide_gas_commands.test.js`; new test files.

**Evidence (2).** Google's Routes API returns no public-transport route in some countries, so the planner wraps Maps in a rail estimator (`planner/index.mjs:73`). For a leg under 1.2 km, or when walking beats the train, it estimates a walk — straight-line distance × 1.25 at 80 m a minute (`planner-rail.mjs:13-17`, `:35`, `:49-50`) — and hands back a stand-in marked `estimated` with the warning "Estimated walk: Google returned no route" (`:118-121`). `fetchLeg` keeps only the minutes, the distance and the line name (`planner-legs.mjs:78`), so the mark and the warning are lost: the day's "transit times on this day are estimates" warning (`planner-day.mjs:138`, `:153`) never fires and the brochure counts the walks as transit. Google does return walking routes there, with route warnings for restricted-use paths. Nothing adds buffers, and an early finish becomes "back early; the rest of the day is free" (`planner-day.mjs:156`).

**Build (2).**
- A leg the planner decides to walk asks Google for a WALK route (through the maps kit's client, ledger and budget guard; at most one extra request per walked leg) and uses Google's minutes and distance with `mode: 'WALK'`. If that request fails, the leg keeps the estimate with `estimated: true` and its warning.
- `fetchLeg` keeps `estimated` (and the warning) on every estimated leg, so the day's estimates warning fires.
- Route character: `footpath` when Google's route warnings mention restricted-use or pedestrian paths. `uphill` and `downhill` come from what can be known without an elevation service (none is available here): the leg ends or starts at a hill-type place (a viewpoint or summit, or a name with "hill", "mount", "-yama", "-san", "peak", "lookout"), or the driving route is much longer than the walk around a footpath (pick the ratio and record it). `trail` marks a hiking path. When a leg is `uphill` or `trail`, ask Google once for a DRIVE route and set `taxi_minutes` ("taxi about 11 min"). Record the extra-request budget per day.
- Buffers: a per-leg `buffer_minutes` rule you pick and record (for example walk 2–5, transit 5 plus 5 per transfer, uphill +5, capped at 15). The schedule includes it (the next stop starts at the leg's arrival plus its buffer). `spare_minutes` per day is the day's unscheduled time after buffers.
- Display: the day card (`tgCmdDayMessages`) shows "walk 22 min", "about 20 min walk (estimate)", "uphill · taxi about 11 min", "+5 min spare", a day line "Spare time: 1 h 10 min", and the booking lines from `tgBkDayLines(trip, day)` when that function exists (`typeof tgBkDayLines === 'function'`; WP-10a defines it). The brochure's legs and its at-a-glance footer count walks as walks and mark estimates. Both read the C10 fields and fall back cleanly for a digest that has none.

**Build (5)** — the six fixes in the Done-when table:
- (a) Place notes are written before the plan, so a note can say "arrive at opening" for a stop the schedule puts at noon. Write a pure guard, exported from a stable module path so the private repo's digest builder can use it too, that drops (or rewrites) the timing advice in a note when the stop's scheduled time contradicts it — opening, first-thing or early-morning advice on a late arrival; sunset or evening advice on a morning stop; a "before X" or "after X" the time breaks — and apply it wherever the brochure shows a stop's note.
- (b) Places Google types as Buddhist temples become `church` (`gems/gems-record.mjs:31`), which defaults to 30 minutes and counts as indoor; a tea ceremony became a 90-minute "neighbourhood". Add `temple`, `shrine`, `garden` and `experience`: the category list, the Google-type mapping, default lengths (`estimator-defaults.mjs:6`; pick sensible ones and record them) and indoor/outdoor. Refine stored legacy `church` records whose name reads as a temple or shrine when they are loaded.
- (c) Rain swaps treat restaurants, cafés, bars and `church` as indoor (`planner/planner-rain.mjs:14`), so a meal can be offered "instead of" a garden. Swaps name covered sights only (museums, galleries, indoor markets, experiences that are not meals…); no swap is better than a meal.
- (d) A pick that does not fit is dropped as "no room left" (`planner-day.mjs:72`, `:77`). Say how many minutes it was short and, when a visit of at least the category's sensible minimum fits, offer it ("a 45-minute visit would fit after <stop>") in the warning and the Later reason.
- (e) A place whose opening days are irregular (its own record says so) or unknown (Google gave no hours) is dropped as "closed on every day of the trip". Keep it, scheduled within any hours that are known, with `check_on_day` (for example "Opening days vary — check before you go"); add the optional place field you need (for example `opening_days: "irregular"` with a short note) to the place schema.
- (f) `time_style: "about"` on sights; `"exact"` on booked stops, timed experiences, meals with a booking and a stop the schedule puts within 30 minutes of its last entry. The display rounds "about" times to the nearest 15 minutes ("about 11:45", no end time) and keeps exact ones as they are today ("11:50–12:55").

**Tests (10b).** On the invented hill fixture: a walked leg takes the recorded WALK route (minutes, distance, `footpath`); an uphill leg gets `taxi_minutes` from the recorded DRIVE route; a failed WALK request falls back to an estimate that stays marked through `fetchLeg` and raises the day's warning. Buffers in the schedule and `spare_minutes`; the brochure counts walks as walks. Each of (a)–(f) with a failing-before, passing-after pair. The day card shows the new fields and still renders an old day without them. The request count per planned day stays under the budget you record.
