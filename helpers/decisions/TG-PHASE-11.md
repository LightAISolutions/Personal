*Started 2026-10-03 (coordinator Opus 5.5; builders `hb-builder-opus`, Opus 5.5 · high; no Fable, owner's rule). Prompt: `helpers/prompts/TG-PHASE-11.md`. Generic facts only — no ids, URLs, secrets, trip or place data of the owner's.*

## 1 Work-package decisions (wave 1)
- WP-11a: `helpers/decisions/WP-11a.md` — a day override (`start`, `end`, per-date hours, `bags`) shapes only its own date: the first leg leaves the start point at its time with no breakfast, a hard end gets no spill and is reached 10 minutes early, and the bag step is a timeline row (the hotel 15 minutes; a locker 10 to store and 10 to collect; forward and carry are lines); a departure day has no dinner and no extras. Dinner comes from the `dinners` pool: within 1.5 km of the last stop or the night's lodging, open that evening, never `menu.fits: 'no'`, never a place used on another day; the owner's picks first, then the Later list, nearest first; the booking line comes from `trip.bookings`, then the place's own booking facts, and the planner never adds a booking. Evening extras (at most 3, events first) lie within 2 km; sunset uses the NOAA algorithm, checked against published tables and an independent algorithm. A place's own facts win over Google's hours (close, last entry, closed weekdays; its visit length gives `minutes_source: 'official'`), with one info warning when they disagree. Japan visit lengths for temples, shrines, gardens, tea ceremonies and course meals apply only to trips in Japan without a researched length. When the profile asks to avoid crowds, a crowd magnet gets the opening or the late slot, may wait for it, and is never dropped. Every new field appears only for new inputs: the old fixtures plan byte for byte as before.
- WP-11b: `helpers/decisions/WP-11b.md` — C11 trip and place schemas (every new field optional, so old records validate; uniqueness lives in the checks); `facts/` (stale after 90 days, a menu check after 30; a closing-time gap over 15 minutes is a conflict; one line of at most 160 characters per kind); `season/` (usual bloom months by hemisphere, recorded with sources; the tropics never drop; the season sheet's forecast wins both ways); the gem screen's `out_of_season` (activities only, never owner seeds), local favourites (2 or more distinct publishers: floor 3.8, off-track limit ×1.5) and crowd magnets (mass-tourism rank ≤ 10, or a rating count in the pool's top decile and ≥ 2,000). Flags never drop a place.
- WP-11c: `helpers/decisions/WP-11c.md` — the plan-digest and shortlist validators (Node and Apps Script) take every C11 field and still refuse unknown keys; `part` and `parts` travel together and a Later list rides in part 1 only; the day's C11 fields live in the day row's chunked JSON and come back only when present, so old rows load as before; plans in parts are staged in a `DigestParts` tab (one row per chunk), joined in part order, checked as one plan, stored and delivered once; a newer build replaces an older one, parts whose top fields disagree drop the build, and an incomplete build is dropped after 24 hours with one notice to the owner; the day card shows the start, bags, last entry, booking, crowd, dinner, end, evening extras and sunset lines (an old day renders byte for byte as before); `/dates <date> start|end <place> HH:MM`, `hours HH:MM-HH:MM`, `bags hotel|locker|forward|carry [note]` and `clear` are checked before saving and sent to the brain as `trip_update.day_overrides`.
- WP-11d: `helpers/decisions/WP-11d.md` — the brochure kit takes every C11 field (strict about unknown keys) in its existing design, no new fonts or colours, and adds CSS only when a C11 field is used: the real start and end and the bag step are timeline rows; a stop shows its last entry, where its length comes from, its booking line only when not booked, and its crowd note; a booked or researched dinner becomes a card; "This evening" shows sunset (hidden on a day that departs before it) and up to three extras in clock order at their own time; place cards get a facts block with sources, the date checked and a stale mark (90 days, a menu 30); a season page sits after the overview with only the events on trip dates; links are https only. A plan without C11 fields renders the same HTML byte for byte (hashes pinned); the sample grows by the season page.
