# WP-10b — decisions and assumptions (honest travel legs, tidy-up fixes)

Brief: `helpers/prompts/TG-PHASE-10.md` rows WP-10b and Contract C10. State and checks: `helpers/status/WP-10b.md`.

## Process
1. **Ownership.** Only the WP-10b paths are edited. Schema JSON files are edited; `schemas/index.mjs` and `schemas/tour-guide-checks.mjs` are not, and changes there go to the status file as REQUESTs. No version, CHANGELOG, README, SPEC, BUILD-STATE or prompt edits.
2. **Fixtures are invented** (Minibury, Port Sorrel, an invented hill town). No network call; Google is reached only through the recorded fixture responder.

## Fix (b) — new categories
3. **Default lengths (minutes):** temple 45, shrine 30, garden 60, experience 90. Typical visit lengths, an inference; museum 120, church 30 and the rest are unchanged.
4. **Google types:** `buddhist_temple`, `hindu_temple` → temple; `shinto_shrine` → shrine; `garden`, `botanical_garden` → garden (they were park); `cultural_center` → experience. `church`, `mosque`, `synagogue`, `place_of_worship` stay church.
5. **Legacy records are refined when loaded, never rewritten.** `refineCategory(place)` in `planner/planner-category.mjs` (a stable path for the private repo's digest builder): a stored `church` whose name reads as a shrine (`shrine`, `jinja`, `jingu`, `taisha`, `-gu`) becomes shrine, as a temple (`temple`, `pagoda`, `monastery`, `mandir`, `wat`, `-ji`, `-dera`, `-tera`) becomes temple; a `neighbourhood` or `other` whose activity names a set-length session (ceremony, class, workshop, tasting, performance) becomes experience. Shrine is tested before temple. Applied in the planner's `prepare()`, in `isIndoor`/`isCoveredSight`, and in gems' `categoryOf` (for stored `church` only).
6. **Indoor/outdoor:** experience is indoor; temple, shrine and garden were already outdoor.

## Fix (c) — rain swaps
7. **Covered sights:** museum, gallery, aquarium, theatre, cinema, library, church, onsen, spa, bathhouse, arcade, workshop, experience; a market only with `indoor: true`; any other non-meal non-shop place with `indoor: true`. Never restaurant, cafe or bar, never a shop or mall, never `indoor: false`. No covered sight in reach means no swap.
8. **Test world:** the planner test world's "Tile Workshop" was a `shop`; it is now a `workshop`, so it stays the covered sight the rain tests expect.

## Fix (a) — the note guard
9. **Module:** `planner/planner-notes.mjs` — `guardNote(text, {arrive, depart, window})`, `guardNoteFields(note, visits, keys)`, `noteConflict`, `clockOf`, `NOTE_RULES`; re-exported from `planner/index.mjs`. Pure, a stable path for the private repo.
10. **Drop, never rewrite.** A sentence whose timing advice the schedule contradicts is left out; the other sentences stay word for word; an emptied field is removed. Rewriting advice would invent text the research never said.
11. **Thresholds (`NOTE_RULES`):** opening / first-thing advice holds up to 30 minutes after the window opens (no window: an arrival before 10:00); early-morning / sunrise / "go early" needs an arrival before 10:00; morning advice an arrival before 12:00; afternoon advice a visit running past 12:00 (late afternoon past 15:00); sunset / evening / night / after-dark / lit-up advice a visit running to 16:00 or later; "before X" breaks when the arrival is at or after X, "after X" when the arrival is before X. A bare hour below 8 with no am/pm is read as pm ("before 5" = 17:00); "noon"/"midday" = 12:00.
12. **Hours are not advice.** "before X"/"after X" is ignored when the words before it in the sentence talk about hours (opens, closes, closing, last entry, open daily, hours), so "closes before 17:00" never removes a sentence.
13. **Where it applies:** every brochure place card's note text fields (why_you, what_to_do, what_to_skip, best_time, tickets, accessibility, food), checked against every scheduled visit of that place (a sentence any visit contradicts goes). The GAS day card gets a port for `note_line` (stops in the digest carry no window, so the no-window rule applies there).

## Suggestion 2 — honest travel legs
14. **A walked leg takes Google's WALK route.** On a TRANSIT day a leg the rail estimator walks gets one `computeRoutes` WALK request (minutes, distance, warnings, a walking Maps link). A WALK request that fails keeps the estimator's distance walk, now `mode: 'WALK', estimated: true, estimate_basis: 'distance'`, and the day gets the one `transit_estimated` warning ("Estimated walk: Google returned no route…", `WALK_ESTIMATED_TEXT`). Train stand-ins keep `mode: 'TRANSIT', estimated: true`.
15. **Flags** (`routeFlags`, `FLAG_ORDER` footpath, trail, uphill, downhill): `footpath` when a Route warning matches `FOOTPATH_RE` (restricted, pedestrian-only/path/way/walkway/street, footpath, walkway, stairs, steps, private road) and is not Google's own beta notice (`WALK_NOTICE_RE`: beta, missing, use caution); `trail` when an end is a hike/trail or a warning names a trail or hike; `uphill` when the destination is a hill point and the origin is not, `downhill` the reverse. A hill point is a viewpoint, hike or trail, or any non-food non-shop place whose name reads as a hill (hill, mount, mt., -yama, -san, peak, lookout, summit).
16. **No elevation service.** Routes responses carry no elevation (checked below), and an Elevation API call per leg would be a new SKU. Hilly is inferred instead: a footpath walk whose DRIVE route is at least `HILL_RATIO` = 1.8 times longer is flagged uphill and downhill (the road winds round what the path climbs). An inference, not a measurement.
17. **Taxi time:** one DRIVE `computeRoutes` per walked leg that is uphill, on a trail, or a footpath that needs the hill test; `taxi_minutes` is Google's driving minutes, set only for uphill or trail legs. No taxi fare is guessed.
18. **Buffers (`BUFFER`, planner-buffer.mjs):** walk ≤ 10 min → 2, ≤ 25 min → 3, longer → 5; transit 5 + 5 per change of line; drive 5; +5 when uphill or on a trail; at most 15. The return leg to the lodging has none. Before a booked stop the buffer shrinks to the slack there is; a booking keeps its time.
19. **Spare time:** `spare_minutes` = the day's waits before the next item plus the time left before `day_end`, after buffers (the sum of the day's `free` blocks, capped at the day's end).
20. **Request budget per planned day:** `route_calls` (one Compute Routes per leg, as before) + `extra_calls` = `extraCallsFor(mode, legs)` = min(12, 2 × legs) on a TRANSIT day (a WALK and a DRIVE per walked leg at most), min(12, 1 × legs) on a WALK day (a DRIVE per hill/trail/footpath leg at most), 0 on a DRIVE day. `LEG_EXTRA.MAX_PER_DAY` = 12; past it a leg keeps its estimate and gets no taxi time. All extra requests go through the maps kit client, ledger and ceiling, under the Compute Routes (Essentials) SKU; `estimateBudget` counts them before any call. Test: the per-day ledger units stay ≤ `route_calls + extra_calls`.
21. **Fixture:** `fixtures/hill-town/` — an invented hill town (invented names, coordinates, dates 2027-09-14…, zone), Google returning no TRANSIT routes, recorded WALK routes with path warnings and recorded DRIVE routes; a hill shrine and lookout.

## Fixes (d), (e), (f)
22. **(d) Shortfall:** a pick dropped as `day_full` is tried at every slot of its day with the longest visit that still fits (binary search over the real `retime`, matrix travel as the fallback, so no extra Maps request). The Later reason and an info day warning say "N min short; a M-minute visit would fit after <stop>". M is offered only when the place is not booked or fixed, its category has a minimum visit, and M ≥ that minimum (rounded down to 5). `MIN_VISIT` (minutes): museum 45, gallery 30, aquarium 45, market 30, viewpoint 15, park 20, garden 30, temple 20, shrine 15, church 15, neighbourhood 30, hike 90, restaurant 45, cafe 20, bar 30, shop 15, other 30, experience none (a session has a set length).
23. **(e) Irregular opening days:** a place is irregular when its own record says `opening_days: 'irregular'` (new optional place field, with `opening_note` ≤ 160) or Google's weekday text matches `IRREGULAR_LINE_RE` (irregular, varies, variable, hours might differ, by appointment, seasonal, check the website/online). Such a place is no longer dropped as closed on every trip day: it is scheduled in the hours Google lists for the first weekday that has any (Monday first) and carries `check_on_day` (its `opening_note`, else "Opening days vary — check before you go"). A place with no hours at all keeps the Phase 9 behaviour and now also carries "Opening hours unknown — check before you go".
24. **(f) time_style:** `exact` for a booking, a fixed stop, an `experience`, or a visit that starts within 30 minutes of the assumed last entry (30 minutes before a known closing time — no last-entry data exists, an assumption); otherwise `about`. Display: an `about` time is the arrival rounded to the nearest 15 minutes with no end time ("about 11:45"); exact stays "11:50–12:55". The plan keeps the real minute; only the display rounds.

## Display (day card and brochure)
25. **Day card wording.** A leg carrying any C10 field reads "walk 22 min"; an estimate "about 20 min walk (estimate)"; then flags, "taxi about 11 min" and "+5 min spare", joined by " · ". A leg without C10 fields (an older digest) keeps "9 min walk", so an old day reads exactly as before. "Spare time: 1 h 10 min" (or "none") follows the last leg on a day with stops; a stop's `check_on_day` is a "🕑" line under it; booking lines from `tgBkDayLines(trip, day)` (WP-10a) follow the warnings, only when that function exists, one line each, non-strings skipped. The note guard runs on each `note_line` against the stop's arrive/depart (`tgCmdDayNoteGuard`, a port tested against the module on the shared table).
26. **Brochure.** The kit schema gains the same optional fields (leg `estimated`, `flags`, `taxi_minutes`, `buffer_minutes`; stop `time_style`, `check_on_day`; day `spare_minutes`). The rail shows "about … (estimate)" and the leg extras; an `about` stop shows "about" over the rounded time; the day stats gain "Spare" and mark "On foot"/"Transit" ", some estimated"; the at-a-glance footer does the same. Walks were already counted by mode: the fault was upstream (estimated walks labelled TRANSIT), fixed in the planner.
27. **Google's walking notice.** Wherever a Google WALK (or BICYCLE) route is shown — the day card and each brochure day with such a leg — the line "Walking routes from Google are in beta and may be missing sidewalks or footpaths." is shown (Google requires it, see below). An estimated walk is not a Google route and carries no notice. An older day with a walk leg now shows the notice too: the obligation predates Phase 10.
