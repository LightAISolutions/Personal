## 4 Probes (coordinator, invented data)
- **Rail.** `rideMinutes` gives 8 km → 31, 15 → 49, 25 → 50, 35 → 62, 40 → 68 and 41 → 70; Scout's `estimateReach` gives 64 minutes by transit for two stations 35 km apart.
- **Day trip, the engine.** Fourteen candidates from an invented base, ranked for a 90-minute limit on a trip day: the left-out list runs every screen in order (a duplicate name in capitals, no rail, too far, closed on the day, out of season); eight items numbered by score, one more; the payload passes the pack's validator and the envelope check; a kept trip's outline day is a full day in a 3 km circle with three of four stops as anchors.
- **Day trip, the core.** After a plan digest of four days, the board is stored once and its card sent: the header with the base, the limit and the day; eight lines with ride and length, the reason and the season line; "…and 1 more within reach"; eight ➕ buttons and 📱. A keep answers Kept and asks "Put Ashcombe on a day?" with the four days; a day tap answers "Replanning day 2" and opens one replan for that date with `daytrip: { board, n: 1 }`. `state.json` → `daytrips_kept` carries the kept trip with its date, base, ride, stops and place id. The four app ops answer: the list with its board and kept trip, the board with `more`, a second keep, and a new request from another base at 60 minutes.
- **What's on, the words and the engine.** `/whatson`'s words read a place with a window, and a place alone (the window then comes from the trip); a window over 31 days, a reversed one, a past one and an over-long place are refused. A year-less date means its next occurrence, so a day already past this year reads as next year's (as designed; the past refusal is for a dated window). From eight finds the engine kept an exhibition running the whole window, an evening light-up over two days, a one-day market and a holiday, sorted by first day, and left out a duplicate in capitals, a find without a page, one outside the dates and one sold out; the payload passed the pack's validator and the envelope check.
- **What's on, the core.** After a plan digest of four days, the board was stored once and one card sent: "All these days" first (the exhibition), then a heading per first day, a ➕ for each choosable item (none for the holiday) and 📱. ➕ on the light-up asked which day (its two days); a day answered Chosen and "Day 3 is already planned — re-plan it to fit this in?" with 🔁, which opened one replan for that date with `whatson { board, item }`. The one-day market was chosen at once and a second tap removed it; the holiday is for information only. `state.json` → `whatson_chosen` held the light-up for its day, and nothing else. A weekly board with nothing new stayed silent; one with a new concert sent "New on in Brackwater" with only that item. That weekly board's `chosen` count read 0 while the light-up was chosen on the earlier board, which led to the coordinator's change in §3; after it, 1.
- **The weekly check.** For a trip from 6 to 9 July the first check fell on 15 June at 09:00 in the trip's zone, through the one alarm trigger; it opened a quiet request with `auto: true` for the trip's window and recorded the day, and the next fell a week later. While its request was open the check waited an hour; `/whatson auto off` left no check, and on again brought it back.
- **The app.** `whatson.list` with the heads, `whatson.get` with the trip's four planned days, `whatson.choose` without a day refused `which_day` with the four days and chose with one, and `whatson.new` opened a request for another place and window.
- **The evening.** With the light-up chosen for 8 July and an exhibition (daytime) chosen for 6 July, the planner offered the light-up first on the 8th only, with `chosen: true`, and never offered the exhibition as an evening; an unchosen open day that was under way when the day's stops finished was offered from 16:15, not from its 10:00 start. The chat showed "This evening" with ⭐ for the light-up and ✨ for the rest.
