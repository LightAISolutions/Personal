# WP-3b — Planner + solver: decisions

Every default the planner chose, with why. Numbers are constants in the named files; change them there.

1. **Exact solver, not a heuristic** (`planner-solve.mjs`). Days have ≤ 12 stops, so Held-Karp over subsets × last stop × lunch flag (≤ 2¹²·13·2 ≈ 106k states) runs in milliseconds and is provably optimal for its objective. Google's `optimizeWaypointOrder` ignores opening hours and bookings, so it is a cross-check only (plan §5.4).
2. **Objective**: maximise Σ weight(priority) with weights 1 → 100, 2 → 10, 3 → 1 (one must-see outranks ten nice-to-haves), then the earliest return to the lodging, then the lowest subset index (determinism). Travel time is not minimised directly; a shorter tour returns earlier, so it is the tie-break.
3. **Lunch is mandatory** on any day whose timeline runs past 14:00 (`LUNCH_WINDOW` 12:00–14:00 in `planner-input.mjs`); a day back by 14:00 eats at the lodging. A day that cannot fit lunch drops stops rather than skipping it. Lunch is a zero-travel node "where you stand" (at the last stop, or the lodging before the first). Length by pace: relaxed 75 / normal 60 / packed 45 min (`PACE`).
4. **Breakfast at the lodging** from `day_start`, 45 / 35 / 25 min by pace, unless `profile.meals.breakfast_at_lodging === false`. Departure = `day_start` + breakfast.
5. **Dinner** is a suggestion, not a scheduled stop: starts at max(return + 30 min, 18:30), length by pace, `at: 'lodging'`; omitted when it would end after 23:00. The night market of the fixture therefore lands in *Didn't fit* (`outside_day`), as the phase prompt expects, rather than being appended to the evening.
6. **Day bounds**: every stop ends by `day_end`; only the return leg may spill, up to `maxSpill` = 90 min, with an `over_long_day` warning. Beyond that the subset is infeasible.
7. **Waiting**: a stop may wait up to `maxWait` = 75 min for its window or booking (the wait becomes a `free` block). Longer waits make that transition infeasible so another order or subset is chosen.
8. **Opening hours** (`planner-hours.mjs`) come from the snapshot's `periods` (Google days, 0 = Sunday), with overnight periods closing past 24:00 and the tail of the previous night kept; a period without `close` = open 24 hours; `business_status` other than OPERATIONAL = `closed_business`; no periods and no weekday lines = `unknown`, which **schedules** the place (no window) with an `hours_unknown` info warning; weekday lines alone are only used to detect "Closed".
9. **Day assignment** (`planner-assign.mjs`) is greedy and deterministic: bookings first (pinned to their date), then promoted places (`scheduled_hint`), then priority 1 → 3, seeded tie-breaks. Score = 0.5 · km to the day's anchor (centroid of its two lodging endpoints) + mean km to the stops already on that day (or the anchor km when none) + 1.5 per stop already there. Geography keeps a day compact; the load term spreads a one-lodging city trip over its days.
10. **Capacity** (`CAP`): a TRANSIT day holds ≤ 10 matrix points (stops + distinct lodging endpoints: 9 stops with one lodging, 8 with two) so its matrix is one request under the 100-element cap; other modes ≤ 12 stops (solver limit). Overflow → `day_full` with `from_date` = the nearest feasible date.
11. **Too far** (`FAR_KM`): a place farther than 30 km (TRANSIT) / 12 km (WALK) / 120 km (DRIVE) from every day's lodging anchor goes to *Didn't fit* as `too_far` before any matrix element is spent on it.
12. **Later reasons** when a place fits no date: `closed_business` > `outside_hours` (windows overlap the day but are too short) > `outside_day` (opens only outside `day_start`–`day_end`, or the booking is outside the day) > `closed_day` (closed on every trip date). No location on the snapshot → `other`. Reason texts name the place and the fact.
13. **One Route Matrix per day**, origins = destinations = the day's points (the diagonal is filled with 0 locally, not asked for), `departureTime` = the day's departure; TRANSIT passes `trip.transit_preferences`. No `routingPreference` is ever sent, so every matrix and leg bills Essentials; only the cross-check bills Pro (one request per DRIVE / WALK day with ≥ 2 stops).
14. **Real legs** are fetched pair by pair at the solver's planned departure time and cached per (from, to) within the day; the timeline is rebuilt on them. If a stop no longer fits, the solver runs once more with the real times in its table; if it still fails, the failing stop is dropped (`day_full`) and the loop repeats. Legs on the wire are `source: 'route'`; the matrix is never shown as a leg.
15. **Cross-check** only where Google can answer it: DRIVE / WALK, ≥ 2 intermediates. Disagreement → `order_disagreement` **info** warning (our order honours hours and bookings). TRANSIT days record `asked: false`.
16. **Maps links**: every leg gets `directionsUrl` (place ids for places, coordinates for a lodging without one); `day_url` is `dayUrl` for DRIVE / WALK with ≤ 9 intermediates, otherwise null (Maps ignores extra waypoints; transit links take none).
17. **Visit lengths** come from the estimator's `chooseMinutes` (`../estimator/index.mjs`, imported lazily) with pace, the profile's interest for the category and the calibration state; `input.chooseMinutes` overrides it (tests and callers with their own policy).
18. **Later lists are built in the planner**, not through the Later kit's `createLists/addItem`, so the planner has no runtime dependency on another WP; names and shapes match the contract ("Didn't fit", "Next time"). `replanDays` keeps previous items for places outside its pool and re-decides the pool.
19. **Budget first** (`planner-budget.mjs`): matrix elements = P² per day, legs = stops + 1 per day (upper bound; re-solves reuse legs), Pro = 1 per DRIVE / WALK day with ≥ 2 stops; `usd_estimate` from the SKU table against the ledger's month; `within_ceiling` against `ledger.remaining`. `planTrip` throws `PlanBudgetError` (code `PLAN_BUDGET`) before any call unless `allowOverBudget`.
20. **Determinism**: `seed` (default 1) feeds a mulberry32 PRNG used only for assignment tie-breaks; the solver and timeline are deterministic. Same input + seed = identical Plan (tested).
21. **Place statuses** on output: scheduled → `scheduled`, in any Later list → `saved-for-later`, everything else unchanged; `scheduled_hint` is consumed and removed (it is not in the Place schema). `rejected` places are ignored; `saved-for-later` places are listed under *Next time* (`owner`) and never scheduled until promoted.
22. **Time zone**: all clock values are local to `trip.timezone`; ISO departure times for the Routes API are computed with Intl (DST-safe); a calendar date's weekday is zone-independent.
23. **`theme`** is the day's distinct stop categories (≤ 3), capitalised — a cheap headline for the brochure day page.
24. **Limits** (recorded, not enforced beyond the checks above): ≤ 31 days, modes TRANSIT / DRIVE / WALK (BICYCLE would work in the kit but is not a trip mode), one lodging per night, places need a snapshot with a location, no multi-day or overnight stops, no second matrix per day (times of day are approximated by the departure-time matrix; the real legs correct them).
